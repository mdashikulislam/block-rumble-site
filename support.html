Block Rumble Support

Puzzle game for iPhone and iPad

How to play

A wooden wall rises from below with a crack running through it. Drag to aim the next block over the crack and let go to drop it. Fill a whole row to clear it. Loose blocks fall after a clear and can fill the next row for a chain. The run ends when the wall reaches the top.

Settings

Tap the gear to turn sound and vibration on or off, reset your best score, or read the credits. The game is quiet when your phone's silent switch is on.
