Block Rumble Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

Block Rumble is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. It does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting service and no in-app purchases. It does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

The app saves your best score, the number of games you have played and your sound and vibration settings, so they are there next time you play. This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it, and you can reset your best score at any time in the app's settings.

Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
